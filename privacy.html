Privacy Policy

This OAuth application is used exclusively by its owner for personal, automated single-user file synchronization and cold archive backups via rclone.

  • Only the owner's authorized storage scopes (drive.file) are accessed to upload and manage backup archives created by the tool itself.
  • No user data is collected, analyzed, sold, or shared with any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.
